# RevealRepair3D claim-evidence matrix

| Proposed claim | Status | Direct evidence | Permissible wording | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No direct prior method was found for the full five-axis task | Conditional | Five-pass academic search, exact-phrase web search, and closest-prior matrix | “No direct match was found in our recorded search through 2026-09-03.” | Do not say nobody has done it or claim an unqualified first. |
| The broad visibility-aware layout idea is novel | Rejected | Make It Home, cinematography, museum/signage, path/isovist, Roomer, ScenePilot, and HOG-Layout overlap | State that the contribution is the conjunction of existing-layout repair, fixed route, signed schedule, attribution, and hard preservation. | Do not claim first visibility-aware optimizer or first 3D scene repair. |
| RevealRepair3D reduces signed schedule loss | Supported | 480 paired cases; mean 0.000164 versus 0.008586 for generic beam; paired cluster CI [-0.011586, -0.005350] | “Reduced signed schedule loss relative to every tested baseline on RouteRevealBench.” | Restrict to tested benchmark, action set, and budget. |
| RevealRepair3D satisfies the declared tolerance reliably | Supported | 476/480 = 99.17%; cluster CI [98.33%, 99.79%] | “Satisfied loss <= 0.005 in 99.17% of benchmark cases.” | Use “tolerance satisfaction,” not perceptual success. |
| The method preserves encoded hard constraints | Supported | 480/480 hard-valid; robustness 618/618 hard-valid re-evaluations | “Preserved all encoded hard constraints in the evaluated cases.” | Does not certify every real-world safety constraint. |
| Signed hide/show modeling is needed | Supported | Visibility-only leakage 0.096225 versus 0.000328; paired cluster difference [-0.113447, -0.079170] | “Unsigned visibility maximization introduces premature-reveal leakage that the signed objective avoids.” | This is geometric leakage, not human suspense. |
| First-hit attribution improves search efficiency | Supported within comparison | Median 99 evaluations versus 480 for generic beam; matched-success median difference -196; 41 ours-only tolerance cases | “Attribution reaches lower loss with fewer evaluations than generic beam under the shared budget.” | Do not claim optimal computational complexity. |
| The method always uses the minimum edit | Not supported | Search ranks loss before cost; no-sparsity ablation is null | “Median output edits = 1; successful edit cost is a feasible debt upper bound.” | Do not call every result minimum-change or exact narrative visibility debt. |
| Sparse-cost tie-breaking causes the gain | Not supported | No-sparsity ablation is outcome-identical on 105 matched cases | Report the null ablation explicitly. | Do not present sparsity as an empirically isolated causal component. |
| Both target and partition edit scopes matter | Supported | Targets-only 69.52%; partitions-only 58.10%; full matched 97.14% | “Restricting either edit scope materially reduces tolerance satisfaction.” | Applies to the constructed fault families. |
| The hard filter is necessary for constraint preservation | Supported within subset | No-hard-filter hard-valid 98.10% versus full 100%; loss is unchanged | “The hard filter prevents invalid outputs without being credited for lower signed loss.” | Do not claim it improves the objective value. |
| Results transfer beyond procedural layouts | Supported narrowly | Procedural 99.33%; accepted Qwen layouts 98.89% | “Performance transfers to a balanced split of Qwen-generated structured layouts.” | Not arbitrary generators, meshes, or real museums. |
| Results are robust to denser visibility sampling and route noise | Supported within tested ranges | 93.20% under joint dense sampling; 82.52% under 10 cm noise; 100% hard-valid | “Remains above the preregistered robustness gates under the tested perturbations.” | No claim beyond 10 cm or different sensor/path models. |
| The result is not a lucky stochastic-baseline draw | Supported on sensitivity subset | 30 strata, five repeats; random best-of-five 80.00%, annealing 63.33%, ours 100%; no strict baseline wins | “The ordering persists under five stochastic repetitions on the fixed subset.” | This is a post-hoc sensitivity analysis, not the primary test. |
| The method is globally optimal | Rejected | Only no-op plus one-action exhaustive enumeration was run | “Matches the restricted oracle in loss and cost for 40/47 one-edit outputs.” | Do not infer multi-step or continuous-space global optimality. |
| The benchmark has no grid overlap | Qualified | Eight compound cases contain one on-grid subfault; no complete inverse is on-grid; exclusion result 99.15% | Disclose both the partial overlap and exclusion sensitivity. | Do not say every subfault magnitude is off-grid. |
| The method improves human narrative or aesthetics | Not evaluated | No human ratings by design | “Optimizes geometric compliance with a signed visibility schedule.” | Do not equate objective compliance with experience, preference, or curatorial quality. |
