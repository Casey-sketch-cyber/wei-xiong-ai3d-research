## Evidence boundary

The scan supports a risk-aware novelty verdict, not a proof that no unpublished,
unindexed, non-English, or newly accepted work exists. The `PARTIAL-OPEN` label
therefore means that no direct match was found in the documented search space;
it does not mean absolute global uniqueness.
