# JCDE cover letter text

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider our manuscript, “Cross-style robustness in image-to-3D generation is model-specific: A paired four-model benchmark with known 3D ground truth,” for publication as a Research Article in the Journal of Computational Design and Engineering.

The manuscript presents a controlled benchmark of TripoSR, InstantMesh, Hunyuan3D 2.1, and TRELLIS using 120 public scanned objects, one photo-like reference, and five matched abstract depictions. All 6,240 scheduled reconstructions completed and were evaluated against repaired watertight ground truth using surface, solid-occupancy, topology, and independent six-view render diagnostics. The pre-specified universal sketch robustness-gap proposition was not confirmed in any of the four models after Holm correction. Instead, the results show a strong model-by-style interaction and support a narrower conclusion: cross-style geometry robustness is a property of a model-style pairing. We believe this result is relevant to computational design workflows that increasingly use sketches and illustrations as image-to-3D inputs.
